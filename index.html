About

I am a Senior Research Engineer in the Autonomous Driving Development division at Hyundai Motor Company, with 7+ years of experience spanning robotics research and production-grade autonomous platforms.

My work centers on reinforcement learning, Transformer-based end-to-end planning, and high-reliability decision-making for self-driving vehicles. I architect closed-loop simulation environments, build LiDAR-based perception and 3D reconstruction systems, and take algorithms all the way onto real vehicles.

Previously, I built South Korea's first large-scale, high-precision LiDAR dataset for autonomous driving (1M+ labeled objects) at KITECH, and designed LiDAR simulation frameworks for sensor development, HD mapping, and high-fidelity 3D scanning. My academic foundation is in T-S fuzzy-based robust control and state estimation from Yonsei University.
